Quality Process

Multi-Point Inspection at Every Stage

No batch moves to the next stage without passing the previous checkpoint. This gate-based system eliminates defect propagation before it starts.

STAGE 01

Incoming Material

Chemical composition & mechanical property verification of all raw wire rod before production commences

STAGE 02

In-Process Check

Dimensional sampling every 30 minutes during cold heading and thread rolling operations

STAGE 03

Post-Heat Treatment

Hardness and hydrogen embrittlement testing after heat treatment per IS 1367 Part 17

STAGE 04

Surface Finish

Coating thickness and adhesion tests on plated / galvanised fasteners; salt-spray exposure validation

STAGE 05

Pre-Dispatch Audit

Final AQL sampling, visual inspection, count verification, and test certificate generation before dispatch

Test Certificates With Every Order

Every shipment from Aggarwal Industries is accompanied by a material test report (MTR) and inspection certificate documenting the specific batch, heat number, dimensional results, and mechanical test values — giving your quality team full traceability.
