Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Buyers

Everything you need to know before placing an order with MTK Screws.

What is MTK Screws?

MTK Screws is the fastener brand of Aggarwal Industries, an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer and trader of industrial stainless steel fasteners based in Kasna Industrial Area, Greater Noida, India. Founded in 1983, MTK Screws manufactures machine screws and self-tapping screws in-house and trades self-drilling screws, rivets, allen bolts, and anchors — supplying 4000+ clients pan-India from a single source.

Which is the best screw manufacturer in India?

The right fastener manufacturer depends on your material grade, standards, and volume needs — but a few things set MTK Screws (Aggarwal Industries) apart: 40+ years of manufacturing experience since 1983, ISO 9001:2015 certification, in-house production of stainless steel machine screws and self-tapping screws, a 99.8% quality pass rate across 15+ QC checkpoints, and 1000+ metric tons/month capacity serving 4000+ clients. Combined with a stocked trading division for self-drilling screws, rivets, allen bolts, and anchors, MTK Screws gives buyers a single source for 52+ fastener products.

What types of fasteners does MTK Screws manufacture?

MTK Screws manufactures precision stainless steel machine screws and self-tapping screws in-house at our Kasna Industrial Area facility. We also trade self-drilling screws, rivets, allen bolts, and structural anchors — giving customers a single supplier for 52 products across 8 categories.

What stainless steel grades are available?

Our manufactured products are available in SS 201, 204, 304, and 316(L). SS 304 is the most popular grade for general industrial applications. SS 316(L) is recommended for marine, chemical, and outdoor environments requiring superior corrosion resistance.

Do you supply custom-size fasteners?

Yes. We manufacture custom fasteners based on customer drawings and specifications. Non-standard thread pitches, special head profiles, unusual lengths, and private labelling are all available. Share your technical drawings with our team at +91 97112 21918 for a quote.

What certifications does Aggarwal Industries hold?

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our products conform to DIN, ISO, ANSI, IS (Bureau of Indian Standards), BS, and JIS standards. Full material test certificates and dimension inspection reports are available on request.

Do you export fasteners outside India?

MTK Screws currently supplies pan-India, delivering to customers across 20+ states. We are actively working toward international exports and global expansion. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
