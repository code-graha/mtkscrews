Our Journey Through Time

A legacy of precision manufacturing that spans four decades of innovation and growth.

1983

The Beginning

The foundation of the business was laid in 1983 when Sushil Kumar Aggarwal established GS Traders as a trading firm in the bustling markets of Chawri Bazar, Old Delhi. Starting with a small team of just 5 people, the company focused on supplying quality industrial products while building long-term relationships based on trust, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

2015

Expansion & Market Growth

In 2015, the business entered a new phase of growth under the leadership of Akshay Kumar Aggarwal, who introduced the MTK Screws brand and began importing high-quality self-drilling screws from China. This strategic expansion strengthened the company's presence in the fastener industry and helped establish MTK Screws as a trusted name in the market.

2023

Manufacturing Revolution

The year 2023 marked a major milestone when Shray Aggarwal joined GS Traders with a vision to move from trading to manufacturing. This led to the establishment of Aggarwal Industries, focused on producing premium-quality precision screws and fasteners. With continuous investment in advanced machinery and infrastructure, by the end of 2026 Aggarwal Industries had successfully acquired and installed 30 sets of modern machines capable of manufacturing a wide range of precision screws using full-gauge quality materials and advanced production processes.

2024 - Present

Industry 4.0 & Beyond

Today, Aggarwal Industries is evolving into a future-ready fastener manufacturing company driven by innovation, technology, and precision engineering. By integrating modern manufacturing practices, automated production systems, and strict quality control standards, the company is committed to delivering durable, high-performance fastening solutions for diverse industrial applications. With a strong focus on quality, scalability, and customer trust, Aggarwal Industries continues to move forward with the vision of becoming a leading name in the precision fastener manufacturing industry.
