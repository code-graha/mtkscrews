FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the questions our customers ask most often.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
For standard stocked sizes, our MOQ is typically 100 pieces or 5 kg — whichever is greater. For custom and non-standard fasteners, there is no fixed MOQ for prototypes and samples. Bulk orders above 50 kg attract volume discounts. Please contact our sales team for your specific requirement.
Do you supply material test certificates (MTC)?
Yes. We provide standard test certificates with every batch. EN 10204 Type 3.1 MTCs (third-party mill certificates) are available on request for critical applications in defence, oil & gas, and export orders. Please mention this requirement when placing your inquiry.
Can you manufacture fasteners from our custom drawings?
Absolutely. We accept DWG, DXF, PDF, and STEP files. Our engineering team reviews your drawing and provides a quote within 24–48 hours. Typical lead time for custom fasteners is 7–15 working days depending on complexity and quantity.
What are your delivery timelines for standard stock?
In-stock items typically dispatch within 1–3 business days. We deliver pan-India via road transport and courier. Orders to NCR/Delhi region are often same-day or next-day. For urgent requirements, contact us directly on WhatsApp for fastest resolution.
